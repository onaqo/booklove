📊 DATA EXTRACTION DETAILS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Homepage Product Structure:
```html
<div class="product-box gym-product" data-pro-id="8555904041113">
  <div class="img-wrapper">
    <div class="front">
      <a href="...">
        <span data-bgset="/cdn/shop/files/image_180x.jpg 180w, ..."></span>
      </a>
    </div>
    <div class="cart-info cart-wrap">
      <form method="post" action="/cart/add">
        <input type="hidden" name="id" value="45823798804633" />
        <button type="submit" class="add_to_cart_btn_cls fly_addtocart">
          <i class="ti-shopping-cart"></i>
        </button>
      </form>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="product-detail">
    <a href="...">
      <h6 itemprop="name">Mythos</h6>
    </a>
    <h4 data-id="8555904041113" data-price="2000">
      $20.00
    </h4>
  </div>
</div>
```

Extraction Logic:
- ID: $container.attr('data-pro-id') → "8555904041113"
- Title: $container.find('h6[itemprop="name"]').text() → "Mythos"
- Price: $container.find('h4[data-price]').attr('data-price') / 100 → 20.00
- Image: $container.find('[data-bgset]').attr('data-bgset').split()[0] → "/cdn/shop/files/image.jpg"
